## 1. What this conversation was really about

This conversation was about turning a rough ecotourism idea for Yaco into a first serious coordination artifact: clear enough to paste into WhatsApp, broad enough to include multiple institutions, and structured enough to become a pilot model rather than a one-off event.

Under the surface, the key move was from “let’s promote an event” to “let’s test whether a community can become tourist-ready through a replicable support process.” The project is not only about bringing visitors to Yaco. It is about learning what minimum infrastructure, coordination, narrative, training, signage, promotion, and evaluation are needed so one community can become a live example for others.

The chat also clarified the role of RVnB: not only as a high-end RV tourism operator, but as a field tester, visual/communication layer, route/signage/design contributor, and eventual bridge to agencies and higher-end packages.
